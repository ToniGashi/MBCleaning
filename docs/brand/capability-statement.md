# MB Cleaning capability statement

Working copy — verify bracketed facts before external use.

## Ready for the next walkthrough

MB Cleaning provides phased construction cleaning for homebuilders and general contractors across Charleston, Summerville, and confirmed surrounding service areas. Our process is designed around construction schedules, clear scopes, and finished homes that present well for inspection and handoff.

## Core services

- Rough cleaning
- Final or finish cleaning
- Touch-up and punch cleaning
- Interior glass and fixture detailing, when included in scope
- Model-home and sales-center cleaning, if confirmed

## How we work

1. **Walk the scope** — Confirm units, phases, access, finished surfaces, and exclusions.
2. **Coordinate the schedule** — Align the crew and site contact around the active build.
3. **Clean by phase** — Complete the approved checklist without blurring scope between visits.
4. **Check the handoff** — Review details, communicate exceptions, and prepare for the next walkthrough.

## Reference prompts

2. How did MB Cleaning communicate around schedule changes?
3. What difference did the team make before walkthrough or turnover?
4. Would you recommend MB Cleaning to another superintendent or builder, and why?

## Scope questions for a new opportunity

- How many units or square feet are involved?
- Which cleaning phases are required?
- What is the projected start date and release cadence?
- Who controls scheduling and site access?
- Which trades may remain active?
- Which surfaces require special care?
- Are utilities, dumpsters, and water available?
- What work is excluded or handled by another trade?
- How are re-cleans and change orders authorized?
- What insurance and vendor documents are required?
