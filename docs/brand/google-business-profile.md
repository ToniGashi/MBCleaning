## Business description

MB Cleaning is owned by American women. We provide construction cleaning for builders, superintendents, and general contractors across Charleston and Summerville, South Carolina. We provide phased new-construction cleaning: rough cleans after active work, detailed final cleans for inspection and turnover, and touch-up cleans after punch work so every home is ready for the walkthrough. We plan each visit around the builder’s schedule, confirm the scope before work begins, and keep one clear point of contact on the jobsite. Our crews have worked in communities across the Lowcountry, including Wisteria at Cane Bay, The Landings at Montague, Coosaw Creek, Oakley Pointe, and Parker’s Preserve. Call or text to plan your next phase.
