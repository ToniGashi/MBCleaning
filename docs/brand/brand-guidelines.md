# MB Cleaning brand guidelines

Version 1.0 · October 2026

## Brand foundation

### Purpose

Help builders move confidently from construction to walkthrough with spaces that look finished, cared for, and ready.

### Vision

Become the Charleston region’s most dependable construction-cleaning partner for residential builders and general contractors.

### Brand promise

**Ready for the next walkthrough.**

### Positioning

MB Cleaning is a construction-cleaning partner for builders, superintendents, and general contractors in the Charleston and Summerville area. We organize work around the build—not a generic housekeeping checklist—so teams get clear phases, responsive communication, and a polished handoff.

### Values

- **Accountability:** Own the scope, the communication, and the final check.
- **Precision:** Notice what affects the walkthrough.
- **Readiness:** Respect the schedule and prepare for the next milestone.
- **Respect:** Work carefully around finished surfaces, active trades, and every jobsite contact.
- **Consistency:** Bring the same standard from the first home to the last.

## Audience

### Primary

**Superintendents and construction managers**

They need crews that arrive when scheduled, understand construction sequencing, flag scope changes, and leave homes ready for inspection. Their risks are missed handoffs, callbacks, damaged finishes, and time lost managing vendors.

### Secondary

- Purchasing and vendor-management teams evaluating scope, credentials, capacity, and price
- General contractors managing closeout on residential or light-commercial projects
- Builders preparing model homes, sales centers, and homeowner handoffs, when those services are confirmed

## Message architecture

### Lead message

Construction cleaning built around your schedule.

### Supporting messages

1. **A clear clean at every phase** — rough, final, and touch-up support aligned to the build.
2. **Communication without chasing** — direct coordination and scope clarity.
3. **Details ready for the walkthrough** — a final presentation that helps the finished work show well.
4. **Local and responsive** — focused on Charleston, Summerville, and confirmed surrounding corridors.

### Proof hierarchy

Use proof in this order:

1. Approved builder or GC references
2. Authentic before/after and finished-project photos
3. Documented service process and quality checks
4. Insurance, safety, capacity, and coverage facts
5. Approved testimonials with name, role, and company

Never substitute vague superlatives for proof.

## Tagline system

### Primary

**Ready for the next walkthrough.**

### Campaign alternatives

- Clean closeouts. Confident handoffs.
- The final detail before the handoff.
- Built for the schedule. Finished for the walkthrough.

Use one line at a time. The primary tagline belongs in high-level brand and footer contexts; campaign lines can support service pages.

## Voice

### Personality

- Direct, not blunt
- Confident, not boastful
- Experienced, not overly technical
- Precise, not fussy
- Helpful, not chatty

### Writing principles

**Lead with the jobsite outcome.**  
Write “Ready for inspection and handoff,” not “We make every space sparkle.”

**Use construction language naturally.**  
Say rough clean, final clean, touch-up, punch list, superintendent, walkthrough, and turnover when accurate.

**Make responsibility clear.**  
Prefer active language: “We confirm the scope before crews arrive.”

**Keep claims measurable.**  
Replace “always on time” with a verified scheduling or response commitment.

**Respect the reader’s time.**  
Use short paragraphs, descriptive headings, and straightforward calls to action.

### Preferred vocabulary

Use:

- Construction cleaning
- Schedule coordination
- Scope
- Phase
- Walkthrough
- Turnover
- Ready
- Finished surfaces
- Quality check
- Jobsite

Avoid:

- Maid service
- Magical
- Sparkling perfection
- Cheapest
- Flawless
- We do it all
- Guaranteed, unless the exact guarantee is documented
- Partnered with [customer], without approval

## Visual identity

### Concept

The visual system combines construction structure with finish-level precision. Strong grids, clipped corners, fine rule lines, and numbered process cues suggest plans and schedules. Warm off-white and brass prevent the brand from feeling industrial or cold.

### Logo direction

Primary wordmark concept:

- Bold “MB” monogram
- Full “MB Cleaning” name
- Optional descriptor: “Construction Cleaning”
- A subtle alignment or check-line device may be used, but avoid roofs, bubbles, brooms, sparkles, and generic house icons.

Until a final vector logo is approved, use the typographic wordmark consistently. Never recreate builder logos or combine MB Cleaning’s mark with a customer’s.

### Color palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Primary | Jobsite Navy | `#102A2E` | Headers, footer, primary surfaces |
| Accent | Safety Brass | `#D7A43A` | Calls to action, rules, highlights |
| Light | Plan Paper | `#F5F1E8` | Main background |
| Surface | Clean White | `#FFFEFA` | Cards and readable content surfaces |
| Text | Charcoal | `#172326` | Body copy |
| Muted | Steel | `#607074` | Secondary copy |
| Support | Field Green | `#2F5D50` | Secondary accents and success states |

Maintain WCAG AA contrast. Safety Brass should not be used for small text on light backgrounds.

### Typography

- **Display:** Geist Sans, 700–900, tight tracking. Use for strong, compact headlines.
- **Body and UI:** Geist Sans, 400–650. Use sentence case.
- **Technical labels:** Geist Mono, 500–600, uppercase with restrained tracking.

Use responsive type sizes and avoid all-caps paragraphs.

### Graphic language

- Fine grid and plan-line details
- Numbered phase markers: 01, 02, 03
- Rectangular cards with one clipped or angled corner
- Controlled use of brass rules and status-like labels
- Simple line icons for phases and contact methods

Do not use glossy gradients, cartoon cleaning icons, stock-image sparkles, or script typography.

### Photography

Prioritize real work:

- Wide, level views of finished rooms
- Detail shots of glass, fixtures, trim, floors, and cabinets
- Crews working safely in branded apparel, with permission
- Paired progress and final images from the same angle
- Natural color, straight verticals, and minimal filters

Do not show unsafe practices, visible homeowner information, builder branding without permission, or identifiable addresses unless approved.

## Service naming

Use these public service labels only after confirming scope:

1. **Rough clean** — construction dust and loose material cleared to prepare for finish work.
2. **Final clean** — detailed cleaning of approved surfaces before inspection or handoff.
3. **Touch-up clean** — focused return after punch work or trade re-entry.

Optional services such as exterior glass, pressure washing, hauling, models, and sales centers should be listed separately with explicit inclusions and exclusions.

## Trust-asset requirements

Collect before launch or publication:

- Current phone and text-capable number
- Business email and canonical domain
- Certificate-of-insurance language approved for public use
- Actual service radius
- Three to six high-resolution project photo sets
- Two approved references or testimonials
- Crew/capacity statement
- Documented rough/final/touch-up scope
- Re-clean and change-order policy
- Written permission for any named customer, logo, subdivision, or project

## Usage checklist

Every MB Cleaning touchpoint should:

- Use “MB Cleaning” consistently
- Lead with construction and turnover outcomes
- Include one clear next step
- Use only current contact details
- Avoid unapproved customer names
- Make scope and qualifiers visible
- Sound dependable, concise, and human
