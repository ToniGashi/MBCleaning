Last reviewed: October 11, 2026

## Executive finding

MB Cleaning has almost no reliable current search footprint. That is a disadvantage for discovery, but it also gives the company room to establish a clean, consistent identity around construction turnover work without having to unwind a large amount of conflicting brand content.

## Local market

Local competitors commonly advertise post-construction cleaning, but most position it as one service within a broad residential/commercial menu.

Examples reviewed:

- [A Man’s Touch](https://www.amanstouchcleaningservice.com/post-construction-cleaning/) — punch-list-to-turnover messaging and phased cleaning.
- [American Crown Cleaning](https://www.americancrowncleaning.com/) — broad premium cleaning with rough, final, touch-up, model-home, and sales-center services.
- [Harbor City Facility Solutions](https://harborcityfacilitysolutions.com/services/post-construction-cleaning/) — operational emphasis on walkthroughs, scheduling, inspections, and credentials.
- [Star Cleaning SC](https://www.starcleaningsc.com/services/post-construction-cleaning) — phased work, HEPA equipment, and veteran-owned positioning.
- [Sabal Palm Services](https://sabalpalmservices.com/post-construction-clean-up/) — builders, GCs, multifamily work, capacity, and deadlines.
- [Go Green Clean](https://www.gogreencleansc.com/construction-cleaning-services) — Summerville-based and eco-focused.

Claims on competitor websites are marketing claims and were not independently verified.

## Buyer language and likely selection criteria

Use terms construction buyers recognize:

- Rough clean
- Final or finish clean
- Touch-up, re-clean, or punch clean
- Turnover and homeowner walk
- Interior glass and label/adhesive removal
- Model-home and sales-center cleaning, only if offered

Likely evaluation criteria:

1. Reliable staffing and schedule coordination
2. Clear scope by phase, including exclusions
3. Direct superintendent communication
4. Quality checks and final walkthroughs
5. Re-clean rules after other trades re-enter
6. Comparable references and authentic project photography
7. Current certificate of insurance and safety practices
8. Capacity to serve active building corridors
