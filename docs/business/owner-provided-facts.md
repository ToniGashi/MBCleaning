### Owner design and copy feedback (October 11, 2026)

- The owner does not like the square grid-line backgrounds. Do not use them.
- After the grid was removed, the owner said the site “looks a little empty.”
- The owner liked the wording “Neighborhoods we know well.” Prefer that phrasing for the
  community showcase heading.
- The owner said the text feels “small overall.” Prefer a larger base type size and more
  readable body/UI copy.
- The owner said the text “looks very robotic.” Write in plain, warm, human language, the way the
  owner would talk to a builder, and avoid jargon such as “release cadence” or “operating rhythm.”
- The owner said the neighborhood house photos used in the page headers were “unhelpful at all.”
  Page photos should show the cleaning work itself (rough clean, final clean, glass, details).
- The owner said the AI work photos of “this guy” looked “a little strange.” Prefer work scenes
  without full people or faces (tools, rooms, supplies; hands-only detail crops are fine).
  Neighborhood exteriors stay only in the neighborhood showcase.
- The owner called out the Process page “A stronger start / What helps us price and schedule
  accurately” section as looking “super weird.” It was replaced with a plain “What to have ready
  when you call” checklist.
