## Preserve owner-provided business information

Before changing MB Cleaning’s public claims, service area, identity, or marketing copy, read
`docs/business/owner-provided-facts.md`.

Whenever the owner provides a new business fact, record it in that file with the date and preserve
the owner’s original wording when a name or detail is uncertain. Do not overwrite owner-provided
facts with assumptions or unrelated online search results.
